# Accessing Immich on iOS

This guide shows you how to set up and use the Immich app on your iPhone or iPad.


ios-serverendpointurl
## Installation

1. Download the [Immich app from the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/immich/id1613945652)

{/* ![iOS App Store](/img/immich/ios/app-store.png) */}

## Part 1: Setting Up the App

1. Open the Immich app
2. When prompted, enter your Immich server URL: `http://photos.jjambrose.info:2283/`

<ResponsiveImage 
  src={ServerEndpointURL}
  alt="ServerEndpointURL" 
  width="450px" 
/>

3. Enter your username and password
4. Tap "Sign In"
:::tip
If this is your first time accessing Immich, you may be prompted to change your password. Follow the instructions on the login page then continue with the steps below.
:::

## Part 2: Uploading Photos to Immich

:::important
You should create an iOS album first, with all desired photos / videos then upload photos to it.
:::
1. Create your iOS album first, with all desired photos / videos.
2. Open the Immich app
3. from the `Photos` pane, click the icon that looks like a Cloud with an up arrow (top right corner)
4. You should see `Backup Albums` this is where you can select the album you created in iOS.
:::note
`Sync albums` is optional, I would reccomend using it but it is not required.
:::
5. Select your album. once selected, just hit back to go to the backup screen.

## Part 3: Uploading Photos to Shared Album

To upload photos to a shared album:


1. From the bottom, click `Albums` 
2. Navigate to the album you want to upload to (I'll be using the "Seattle 2025 🐟" album as an example)
3. "Add Photos +"
