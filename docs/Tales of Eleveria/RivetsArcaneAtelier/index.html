Rivet's Arcane Atelier

The complete collection of magical creations from the workshop of Rivet, Artificer 7 / Rogue 11

info

Atelier - an artist's or designer's studio or workroom

Introduction​

This catalog documents all magical items designed, created, or enhanced by Rivet. From custom innovations blending arcane energy with mechanical engineering to traditional artificer infusions applied to companions' equipment, each entry represents the culmination of study, experimentation, and practical application of magical theory.

Origins

M.E.R.L.I.N. (Magical Entity for Research, Liaison, Interface, and Navigation) was conceived by an ancient and powerful artificer who once led The Astral Silver. This artificer discovered an ancient artifact, the Aetheric Crystal, a relic that could harness and channel vast amounts of arcane energy.

Rivet's Astral Wraps​

Intended Rarity: Rare Attunement: (requires attunement by a Monk)
Development Phase: Theoretical

Concept Description​

These hand and wrist wraps are crafted from pale-blue astral silk, banded with micro-filament threading and inlaid sigils that flicker with Rivet's signature arc light circuitry. They're laced at the seams with crimson trim, a nod to Gio's monastic roots with the Order of St. Denier, while the shimmering body pulses like a still lake under moonlight.

When worn, the wraps ripple faintly with every movement faint streaks of glowing blue trailing behind punches, blocks, and sidesteps like echoes of intent. They hum with barely contained energy, not loud but present, like breath before a strike. These are no common relics. They are tools of discipline, deception, and divine rebellion, engineered in silence and starlight.

Proposed Features​

Astral Impact
Your unarmed strikes deal force damage instead of their normal type.

Ki Blink
When you use Step of the Wind or Flurry of Blows, you may teleport up to 10 feet to an unoccupied space you can see as part of that action.

  • You must end your movement in an unoccupied space.
  • You do not provoke opportunity attacks from that creature during this movement.

Afterimage Drift
When you activate Patient Defense, you gain a +1 bonus to AC until the start of your next turn. Additionally, you may immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. This movement does not count against your movement for the turn.

Astral Convergence (1/day)
As a bonus action, you may cast Mirror Image on yourself.

  • No components are required.
  • This version does not require concentration.
  • The illusory duplicates mimic your erratic monk style, trailing constellations as they shift and shimmer in astral sync with your movements.
