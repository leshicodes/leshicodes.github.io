# Inheritance

WIP


{/* truncate */}


> My mother taught me  
> how to see red  
> without opening my eyes  
> how to track the shift  
> in breath,  
> how to measure silence  
> like it's about to explode.
> 
> Love was loud.  
> Then gone.  
> Then loud again.  
> I learned to hold my breath  
> longer than her storms,  
> to recognize affection  
> by its absence.
> 
> There were names  
> she gave me  
> that don't belong  
> on anyone's child.  
> I carry them  
> in silence  
> so they don't echo forward.
> 
> I do not mirror  
> the mouth that made me.  
> I don't need  
> to become her  
> to understand her.
> 
> I have raised no children,  
> but I end the cycle  
> just the same  
> I would not teach them  
> to brace for love  
> as if it's a blow.
> 
> I am the broken branch  
> that roots itself elsewhere.  
> I grow sideways.  
> I don't reach  
> for what raised me.
> 
> I am the first  
> to unlearn violence  
> and call it love.  
> To place a hand  
> where pain once lived  
> and not let it strike.
> 
> This inheritance  
> dies in me.  
> This legacy  
> goes no further.  
> This name  
> learns to mean  
> something else.
